    How to survive the journey

    Paddle upriver. Steer with ← →, A/D, touch buttons, or drag to avoid logs and whirlpools. Faster pace means more distance and more risk.

    Fish to open the harpoon challenge. Explore for food and field observations. Camera photographs a visible animal. Camp to rest and repair.
